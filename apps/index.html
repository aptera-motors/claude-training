Apps Compared

Same Claude brain. Three different doors in. Here's which one to knock on.

⚡ In 30 seconds

Claude lives in a desktop app, a website, and a phone app. They all chat. But only the desktop app can touch your local files, run Schedules, and power Co-Work connectors like Jira and Ramp. Phone wins when you're out in the world and need a camera.

Same brain, three doors in

No matter which app you open, you're talking to the same Claude — same models, same smarts, same data policies. The difference is what each surface can connect to. Think of it like your work laptop vs. your phone vs. the browser on the conference room TV. Same you, different superpowers available.

Which should I use?

Three personas, three answers — find yourself:

🖥️

The Daily Driver

You're at your desk most of the day. You want Claude in Jira, Confluence, and your file system. You might set up a morning briefing or an end-of-day summary.

→ Desktop app, all day long.

🌐

The Occasional Asker

You don't live in Claude but you want it handy when you need it. No install, no setup — just open a tab and go. Works great for one-off drafts and quick questions.

→ claude.ai in your browser.

📱

On the Road / In the Shop

You're on the floor, at a supplier site, or in a meeting room without your laptop. Snap a photo of a part label, a spec sheet, or a whiteboard and ask Claude about it. Voice mode keeps your hands free.

→ Phone app, no contest.

Heads up: Schedules run locally

Schedules and Routines are local automations — they live on your machine, inside the desktop app. That means they only run while your computer is on and the Claude app is open. Close the laptop, the schedule sleeps. They are not cloud jobs that run in the background on Anthropic's servers. If you need something to fire overnight or on weekends, plan accordingly.

🧪 Get set up in 5 minutes

Install the desktop app and connect one tool you actually use. Pick one from this prompt and follow Claude's instructions:

I just installed the Claude desktop app. Walk me through connecting it to Jira so I can search issues and create tasks without leaving this chat. I'm not technical — give me step-by-step instructions with screenshots described.

Already on desktop? Try the phone app too — snap a photo of something on your desk and ask:

What is this? [photo of component/label/part]. Give me a plain-English explanation of what it does and why it matters.

🔑 Key takeaways

  • All three apps use the same Claude — the difference is what they can connect to and access.
  • Desktop is the power seat: local files, Schedules, and full Co-Work connector support live here.
  • Phone is the field tool — camera input and voice make it uniquely useful away from your desk.
