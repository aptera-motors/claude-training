Getting Started

From zero to having a real conversation with Claude — in under ten minutes.

⚡ In 30 seconds

Aptera provides Claude access to every employee. You can use it in a browser tab right now — no install needed. For more power, the desktop app adds connectors and automation. This page walks you through logging in, saying hello, and running your first useful task.

Need help getting access?

Two people can sort you out fast:

  • Jake (jake@aptera.us) — questions about how we use Claude, what's allowed, or what any of this even means.
  • IT — can't log in, need a seat added, desktop app won't install. IT has the keys.

When in doubt, start with Jake. He likes this stuff.

The three ways in

Pick the one that fits where you are right now. You can always level up later.

🌐

Browser (claude.ai)

Log in with your Aptera account at claude.ai. That's it. Works on any device, no install required. This is where most people start — and honestly, most people stay.

🖥️

Desktop App

Download from claude.ai/download. Unlocks connectors (plug Claude into Jira, SharePoint, Outlook), Co-Work mode, and Schedules for recurring tasks. The upgrade worth making.

💻

Claude Code

A terminal tool for technical folks — engineers, data analysts, anyone comfortable with a command line. IT can help install it. Skip this one if that sentence made your eye twitch.

Your first 10 minutes

Do this right now. It'll take less time than your next Slack scroll.

  1. 1
    Log in at claude.ai

    Use your Aptera email. If you hit a paywall or "no access" screen, ping Jake or IT.

  2. 2
    Say hello

    Type "Hi, I work at Aptera Motors. What can you help me with today?" Watch it respond like a smart colleague who's been briefed on the job.

  3. 3
    Ask it to rewrite an email

    Copy a recent email you sent — something you wished landed better — and ask Claude to punch it up. You'll immediately see what it's good at.

  4. 4
    Notice the Artifact panel

    When Claude produces a document, table, or longer piece of content, it opens in a side panel. You can copy, download, or keep editing right there. That panel is your deliverable.

  5. 5
    Try a follow-up

    Reply in the same conversation: "Make it shorter" or "Add a friendlier sign-off." Iteration is the whole game. The first answer is a draft. The third answer is usually great.

🔒 Keep it clean

Don't paste passwords, API keys, customer PII, or anything you wouldn't put in a Slack message to the whole company. Claude is powerful — but your judgment about what to share is still the first line of defense. Not sure? Check the Safety & Data page.

🧪 Starter prompts — copy and go

Paste either of these into Claude right now to feel what it's like:

Rewrite this email to be friendlier but still firm. Keep it under 150 words. Here's the original: [paste your email]

I'm new to using AI at work. Ask me three questions to figure out which tasks I should try delegating to you first.

🔑 Key takeaways

  • You can start in the browser right now — log in with your Aptera account at claude.ai, no install needed.
  • The desktop app is the real power move: connectors, Co-Work, and Schedules live there.
  • Iteration is the superpower — treat every first answer as a rough draft, then push back until it's right.
