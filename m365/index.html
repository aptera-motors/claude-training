Claude in Microsoft 365

You don't always have to go to Claude. Sometimes Claude comes to you.

⚡ In 30 seconds

Claude plugins live inside Outlook, Word, PowerPoint, and Excel — the apps you're already in all day. No tab-switching, no copy-pasting. Just highlight, click, and get the help you need right where the work is happening.

There's a version of Claude that lives on a tab in your browser. Then there's the version that shows up inside the app you already have open. The M365 plugins are the latter — a Claude sidebar (or right-click menu) embedded directly into the Microsoft tools your day already runs on. Less friction, faster results.

📧 Outlook

Your inbox is not a to-do list. Let Claude help you triage it like one.

Draft replies in your voice

Give Claude the context ("I need to push back on this deadline but stay friendly") and it writes the reply. You edit, you send. Done in 30 seconds.

Summarize long threads

That 47-message chain from last Tuesday? Ask Claude "what do I actually need to do?" and get the action items without reading the whole saga.

Turn chaos into a crisp follow-up

Rambling thread, no clear owner, no next steps. Claude synthesizes it into a clean follow-up email ready to send to the whole group.

Summarize this email thread and tell me: (1) what decisions were made, (2) what I need to do, and (3) if there's anything still unresolved.

📄 Word

Your document, improved — without leaving the document.

Summarize & critique in place

Open a doc, ask Claude for a one-paragraph summary and what's missing. Great for reviewing vendor proposals or policy drafts without reading every word.

Rewrite for a different audience

Engineering spec that needs to become an exec summary? Highlight the section, ask Claude to rewrite it for a non-technical audience. No separate doc needed.

First drafts from outlines

Paste a bullet outline and ask Claude to expand it into a full section. You get a draft that's 80% there — you bring the last 20% of judgment and nuance.

I've pasted an outline below. Expand it into a full draft. Use clear, direct language — no buzzwords. Flag anywhere you're uncertain about the details so I can fill them in.

📊 PowerPoint

Because nobody ever said "I wish I spent more time making slides."

Outline a deck from a doc

Paste a report or brief and ask Claude to propose a slide outline with titles and one-line summaries per slide. Skeleton in seconds, not hours.

Punch up slide headlines

"Q3 Results" is a topic, not a headline. Ask Claude to rewrite your titles as declarative statements that tell the story even if someone only reads the headers.

Speaker notes from slides

Paste your slide content and ask Claude to write speaker notes that anticipate audience questions and hit the key talking points — without sounding like a script.

Here are my slide titles and bullet points. Write speaker notes for each slide — 3–5 sentences, conversational tone, and flag any slide where the content might need more data or a stronger point.

📈 Excel

Formulas are a language. Claude is fluent.

Plain-English formula explainer

Paste any formula — even a nested horror show — and ask Claude to explain it step by step in plain English. Your future self will thank you.

Suggest formulas from a description

"I want to look up a value in another sheet based on two matching columns." Just describe what you need and Claude returns the formula, ready to paste.

Summarize what a worksheet is telling you

Paste a table of data and ask "what's the story here?" Claude spots trends, outliers, and the headline number — handy before you present to the team.

Here's a formula: [paste formula]. Explain exactly what it does, step by step, in plain English. Then tell me if there are any common edge cases or errors I should watch out for.

🧪 Try it in Outlook

Find the longest unread email thread in your inbox. Open it, then open the Claude sidebar in Outlook. Paste the thread (or use the plugin's summarize feature) and send this prompt:

Read this email thread and give me: a one-paragraph summary of what happened, a bullet list of action items with owners (if mentioned), and any open questions that still need a decision.

You should have a clean briefing in under a minute — instead of reading 40 emails.

🔑 Key takeaways

  • Claude plugins live inside Outlook, Word, PowerPoint, and Excel — you don't have to leave the app you're working in.
  • The highest-value moves: summarizing long threads, rewriting for a different audience, and explaining or generating formulas.
  • Plugins are rolling out now — if you don't see them yet, email jake@aptera.us.
