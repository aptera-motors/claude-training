---
question: Which mode should I use — Chat, Co-Work, or Code?
order: 1
---

Use **Chat** for quick thinking, drafting, and one-off questions—no setup needed. **Co-Work** is your jam when you need Claude to see your files and folders; drop in a messy spreadsheet or doc and ask Claude to clean it up or analyze it. **Code** gives Claude full access to your machine and development environment when you're building or debugging. Each has a different vibe—check out [The Three Modes](/claude-training/modes/) for the full breakdown with real examples.
