---
question: Why does Claude seem to forget things mid-conversation?
order: 4.5
---

Within a single chat, Claude holds everything in its **context window** — its short-term working memory for that session. It's large but finite, so in very long conversations (lots of back-and-forth, big documents, many files) it fills up and Claude's recall of earlier details can start to slip. Quick fixes: start a fresh chat when you switch topics, ask Claude to summarize the work so far before continuing, or save important details to a document. For work that spans many sessions, lean on **Memory** and **Projects** so Claude remembers across chats. See the [Context Window](/claude-training/context-window/) and [Memory & Projects](/claude-training/memory/) lessons for the full picture.
