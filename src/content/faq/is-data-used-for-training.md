---
question: Is my data used to train the model?
order: 2
---

No. Aptera uses an enterprise account, so nothing you ask Claude—no conversations, files, or context—goes into training new models. Your data stays yours. That said, treat it like any internal tool: don't paste passwords or secrets you wouldn't share in an email. See [Safety & Data](/claude-training/safety/) for the full data policy.
