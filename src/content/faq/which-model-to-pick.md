---
question: Which model should I pick — Haiku, Sonnet, or Opus?
order: 5
---

**Haiku** is fastest and cheapest—great for quick analysis, summarization, and routine tasks. **Sonnet** is the all-rounder: smart enough for complex work but still fast and cost-effective. **Opus** is the heavyweight: best for really hard problems (deep reasoning, code review, tricky analysis). See [Tokenomics](/claude-training/tokenomics/) for a side-by-side comparison and when each pays for itself.
