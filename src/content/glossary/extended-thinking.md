---
term: Extended thinking
letter: E
---

**Extended thinking** is a mode where Claude reasons longer before answering — it's like the effort dial in Claude Code, but for Chat and Co-Work. More thinking time means better answers on hard problems, but it costs more tokens. Use it when you need Claude to work through something tricky: debugging a thorny issue, designing architecture, or reasoning through a decision with lots of angles. For a quick summary or chat, you don't need it — but for problems that deserve deep work, it's worth the extra tokens. See the <a class="text-gold underline" href="/claude-training/effort/">Effort dial</a> for the full picture.
